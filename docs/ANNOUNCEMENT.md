**What is Viktoria?**
Viktoria is your new AI-powered phone assistant, designed to handle common customer inquiries 24/7. She can answer questions about your hotels, policies, parking, room options, and more—giving your Berlin support team breathing room to focus on complex cases.

**What can she do right now?**
- Answer questions about Dormero Hotel Berlin (parking, rooms, check-in times, pet policy)
- Provide consistent, on-brand responses in the Dormero voice
- Handle multiple calls simultaneously—no hold times

**What do you get?**
- **Control Center Dashboard** — Your support team can review every conversation, see what customers are asking, and rate call quality
- **Analytics** — Track call volume, success rates, and customer satisfaction trends at a glance
- **Feedback Loop** — Each call can be rated, helping us continuously improve Viktoria's responses
